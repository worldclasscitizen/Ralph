# Roadmap

Ralph pursues three goals without treating them as interchangeable: maximize verified quality, reduce time when quality is equivalent, and reduce cost when both quality and time remain equivalent.

## v0.2 beta — current

- Documentation site and explicit maturity matrix
- Quality-first online routing and ergonomic candidate, fixed-route, and Hard Pin controls
- EvidencePacket rehydration and structured guardrail ledger
- Risk-tier verifier, coverage baseline, and benchmark command surface

## v0.3

- Raise line and function coverage toward 80% and branch coverage toward 70% without lowering the enforced floor
- Provider-specific context-window telemetry for safe limited session continuation
- More calibrated runtime escalation and local reliability history
- Benchmark trend and Pareto views in the dashboard

## v0.4

- Broader clean-worktree and mutation strategies across language ecosystems
- Frozen-invariant approval UX and reproducible human calibration workflow
- Critical-path mutation score of at least 80%

## v1.0 gates

- Live provider verification on supported operating systems
- Stable migration, interruption, recovery, and evidence schemas
- Line coverage at least 90% and branch coverage at least 85%; critical policy branches at 100%
- Reproducible benchmark evidence with no qualified-quality regression
- Security, contribution, governance, and release documentation suitable for external maintainers

Parallel feature worktrees and intra-loop waves are intentionally not part of the current milestone. They require a separate task-DAG, merge-queue, conflict, and capacity design.
